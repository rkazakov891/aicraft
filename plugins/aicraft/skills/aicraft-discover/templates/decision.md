# DEC-NNN: Decision question

## Context and affected scenarios

State the question and link its sources, scenarios, and related features.

## Alternatives

Record each viable option, consequences, dependencies, and trade-offs.

## Positions

Record the independent reviewer's recommendation and Discover's position, including remaining disagreement.

## Decision and attribution

Record the operator's choice, reasoning, confirmation source, and time. Leave the choice unresolved until supplied by the operator.

## Supersession

Link the earlier decision this replaces, or the subsequent decision replacing this one. Preserve alternatives and historical reasoning.
